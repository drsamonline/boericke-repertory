How to repertorise

1. In Repertory, search symptoms and tap every rubric that matches the case. 2. Open Repertorisation: remedies are scored across the selected rubrics (grade 2 = 2 points, grade 1 = 1 point) and ranked by total. 3. Fill in the case details and your prescription, then Save repertorisation. Saved cases can be reloaded, printed, exported or imported back as JSON.

Tip: tap any remedy chip inside a rubric to see every rubric that lists it. Press / anywhere to jump to search.

Grade legend

Italic remedy — grade 2: “the more frequently verified clinical remedy” (prefatory note).
Plain remedy — grade 1: listed in the rubric.

Data notes

Remedies are de-duplicated (OCR variants merged into one canonical name) and duplicate rubrics merged. Remedy-less category headings are kept for structure but never appear in search results, so every rubric shown carries at least one remedy.
